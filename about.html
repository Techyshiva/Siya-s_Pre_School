Our Story

Where childhood is celebrated, not rushed.

Siya's Pre-school was born from a simple belief: that the early years of a child's life are the most precious — a season meant for wonder, warmth, and gentle discovery. Tucked along Khadkat Road in Ashti, our little school offers a safe and joyful space where children take their very first steps into learning.

We don't believe in pushing children to grow up faster. Instead, we walk beside them — observing, encouraging, and letting their natural curiosity lead the way. Every song we sing, every story we share, and every craft we create is designed to nurture a confident, kind, and curious little person.

A Message from our Principal

"Every child has a star inside them. Our job is to help it shine."

When parents trust us with their child, they're sharing a piece of their world. We don't take that lightly. At Siya's, we work every day to be a second home — a place full of warmth, gentle voices, and tiny moments of magic.

We focus on the whole child: their mind, their heart, their little wiggling body. Whether they're learning to count with pebbles or comforting a friend during circle time, every experience is a building block. Come, walk through our doors. We can't wait to meet your family.

Mrs. Asha Patil

Founder & Principal, Siya's Pre-school
How We Teach

A blend of Montessori & Play-way magic

Our methodology brings together the best of two beloved approaches — giving children the freedom to explore and the gentle structure to thrive.

01
Hands-on Discovery

Real materials, real textures, real experiences — children learn by doing, not just watching.

02
Self-paced Growth

No racing, no comparing. Each child blossoms on their own beautiful timeline.

03
Learning Through Play

Songs, stories, role-play and games turn every concept into a delightful adventure.

04
Emotional Wellbeing

Kindness, sharing, and self-expression are taught with the same care as ABCs and 123s.
