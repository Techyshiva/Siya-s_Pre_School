Programs that grow with your child.

From their very first steps with us at age 2 to their confident leap into "big school" — we've designed each stage with love.

Age-specific Programs

Find the right nest for your little one

Ages 2 – 2.5
Playgroup

A gentle introduction to a school routine — full of cuddles, colors, and brand-new friends.

  • Sensory & motor play
  • Music & rhymes
  • Toilet training support
  • Socialisation basics
Ages 2.5 – 3.5
Nursery

Curiosity meets structure as little ones explore letters, numbers, and the world around them.

  • Pre-writing skills
  • Storytelling circles
  • Creative arts
  • Outdoor play
Ages 3.5 – 4.5
Junior KG (LKG)

A confident step into early literacy, numeracy, and group learning — through fun, never pressure.

  • Phonics & reading
  • Number sense
  • Hands-on science
  • Show & tell
Ages 4.5 – 5.5
Senior KG (UKG)

Big-school readiness — building confidence, independence and a genuine love for learning.

  • Reading & writing
  • Math & logic
  • Public speaking
  • Project-based learning
Our Safe Spaces

Designed around little hands and big imaginations

Colorful Classrooms

Our classrooms are bright, airy, and thoughtfully arranged with low shelves, learning corners, and child-sized furniture — everything within easy reach for tiny explorers.

  • Child-safe, washable materials
  • Reading nooks with cushions & books
  • Labeled learning corners
  • Plenty of natural light & ventilation

Activity & Sensory Room

A multi-purpose space full of building blocks, musical instruments, role-play props and sensory bins — where children learn through touching, sorting, building and pretending.

  • Music & movement sessions
  • Sensory & messy play
  • Block & construction zone
  • Dramatic role-play corner

Outdoor Play Area

Fresh air, open sky, and laughter on loop. Our enclosed outdoor space features age-appropriate equipment over soft, cushioned flooring — supervised by trained staff at all times.

  • Soft EVA & rubber flooring
  • Slides, swings & climbers
  • Sand & water play stations
  • Shaded resting areas
